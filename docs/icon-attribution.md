The ten original site's icon choices are retained without geometry changes:
X (x-twitter), Bluesky, mixi2 (m), pixiv, Xfolio (regular images), BOOTH
(store), メロンブックス (book), Qiita (q), Zenn (z), GitHub.
Their SVG paths are included locally in `src/components/fontawesome-icons.json`
to avoid a blocking remote icon kit and keep SSR output complete.
